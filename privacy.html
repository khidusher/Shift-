When the site is hosted on Vercel, the hosting and delivery service may process technical request information, such as an IP address and system configuration, to deliver and protect the service. WES should confirm which Vercel features and log-retention settings are enabled for the production project. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice.

Fonts and external links

The page requests its typefaces from Google Fonts. A visitor’s browser connects to Google’s font service to retrieve them; Google necessarily receives the request IP address to transmit the font files. Read Google Fonts’ privacy information.

The site also links to WES’s Instagram profile. Following that link takes you to Instagram, which operates under its own privacy terms. The SHIFT website does not receive information from that visit.
